Privacy Policy

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, and protects information. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information the Bot Collects
The Bot may collect and store limited information necessary for its operation, including:

User IDs

Server IDs

Channel IDs

Message content, but only when required for command execution

Server configuration data (settings, preferences, permissions)

Interaction logs (commands used, timestamps)

The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as real names, emails, or payment data.

3. How Information Is Used
Collected data is used solely for:

Executing bot commands

Maintaining server-specific settings

Improving functionality and performance

Preventing abuse or misuse

Debugging errors or technical issues

The Bot does not sell, rent, or share data with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security
Data may be stored in a secure database or server environment. Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and the Bot owner cannot guarantee absolute protection.

5. Data Retention
Data is retained only as long as necessary for the Bot’s functionality. Logs and temporary data may be periodically cleared. Server administrators may request deletion of stored data at any time.

6. Third‑Party Services
If the Bot integrates with external APIs or services, those services may have their own privacy policies. Users are responsible for reviewing third‑party terms when applicable.

7. Children’s Privacy
The Bot follows Discord’s minimum age requirements. It does not knowingly collect information from users under the age permitted by Discord’s Terms of Service.

8. Your Rights
Server owners or users may request:

Removal of stored data

Clarification on what data is stored

Termination of the Bot’s access to their server

Requests can be made by contacting the Bot owner or administrators.

9. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact
For questions, concerns, or data requests, contact the Bot owner or server administrators.
